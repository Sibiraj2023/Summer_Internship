Sample Journey

A Week in Southeast Asia

Seven days carefully sequenced to balance discovery, rest, and the kind of slow mornings you'll talk about for years.

Day 1 – Arrival

Bangkok, Thailand

Land at Suvarnabhumi, check into a riverside hotel, and ease into the city with an evening cruise along the Chao Phraya. Street food dinner at Yaowarat Road.

Days 2 – 3

Temples & Night Markets

Explore Wat Pho and the Grand Palace in the cooler morning hours. Afternoon tuk-tuk tour of hidden canals. Evenings belong to the floating markets and live music at Khao San.

Day 4 – Flight North

Chiang Mai, Thailand

An hour's flight north and you're in a completely different Thailand — mountain air, night bazaars, and the extraordinary Elephant Nature Park for an ethical wildlife experience.

Days 5 – 6

Luang Prabang, Laos

Rise at dawn to offer alms to the saffron-robed monks processing silently through town. Spend afternoons at Kuang Si waterfalls. Slow evenings on the Mekong.

Day 7 – Farewell

Departure & Reflection

Final morning at a riverside café with a strong Lao coffee. Pack the journals, carry the memories. The journey ends; the stories begin.

Travel Tips That Actually Matter

Before You Fly

  • Check visa requirements 8–12 weeks in advance, not days
  • Travel insurance with medical evacuation is non-negotiable
  • Scan every document and email it to yourself
  • Notify your bank before leaving — card blocks ruin first days
  • Download offline maps (Maps.me / Google Maps) for every stop
  • Learn 10 words in the local language — locals notice and appreciate it

On the Ground

  • Carry a small day-pack; leave valuables in the hotel safe
  • Buy a local SIM at the airport — it's always cheaper than roaming
  • Eat where locals eat at lunch, not where tourists eat at dinner
  • Budget 20% extra for the things you didn't plan
  • Bargain respectfully — aggressive haggling is rarely appreciated
  • Rest days are part of the itinerary, not wasted days

The Essentials Packing Guide

Packing light is a skill. Pack right and you'll move faster, worry less, and never pay an overweight fee again.

🧳 Clothing

  1. 3 lightweight t-shirts (quick-dry fabric)
  2. 2 pairs of versatile trousers
  3. 1 smart outfit for dinners/temples
  4. Compact rain jacket or windbreaker
  5. Comfortable walking shoes
  6. Flip-flops or sandals
  7. Warm layer for flights & cold nights

🔌 Tech & Gear

  1. Universal power adapter
  2. 10,000 mAh portable charger
  3. Noise-cancelling earbuds
  4. Unlocked smartphone
  5. Compact mirrorless camera (optional)
  6. Anti-theft crossbody bag
  7. Waterproof phone pouch

💊 Health & Safety

  1. Prescription meds + 3-day extra supply
  2. High-SPF reef-safe sunscreen
  3. DEET insect repellent
  4. Oral rehydration sachets
  5. Basic first-aid kit (plasters, antiseptic)
  6. Filtered water bottle
  7. Copies of travel insurance & ID
